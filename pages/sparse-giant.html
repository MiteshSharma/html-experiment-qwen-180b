The superpower

It fits a giant onto one machine by refusing to hold its largest tensor in memory.

Sparsity in three dimensions at once — experts, attention, and embeddings — is what makes a model this large behave like a small one.

The 47.68 GiB n-gram embedding table never lives in RAM. It sits on NVMe and is read 16 rows at a time, per token. Only 10 of 512 experts fire for each token. And 36 of the 48 layers use linear attention, so 838,860 KV tokens compress into just 12.29 GiB. None of these tricks is exotic on its own — but stacked together, they collapse a frontier-scale MoE into a footprint that a single desktop-class accelerator can actually carry.

Architecture

48 layers, three kinds of sparsity, one shared brain.

The body alternates linear-attention and full-attention layers, routes every token through a 512-expert MoE, and bolts a per-layer n-gram embedding table (PLE) and a speculative draft head onto the front and back.

Memory vs. disk

Where the 123.5 GiB actually lives.

The whole checkpoint is 123.53 GiB of tensor data, but only 78.16 GiB is resident. The single largest tensor — the 47.68 GiB FP8 PLE table — never enters memory; it is streamed from NVMe 16 rows at a time.

Quantization

76,583 weight tensors, three precisions.

NVIDIA ModelOpt, MIXED_PRECISION, group size 16 — counted straight from the checkpoint index. NVFP4 carries the MoE; FP8 carries the draft head; BF16 is left alone where precision still matters.

NVFP4 4-bit

73,728 tensors · 96.3% — exactly 512 experts × 48 layers × 3 projections. The MoE is where the bits go.

FP8 block-scaled

1,536 tensors · 2.0% — all of them in the MTP draft head (512 experts × 3 projections). The PLE table is FP8 too, but not NVFP4.

BF16 unquantized

1,319 tensors · 1.7% — attention, norms, embeddings, and the vision tower. Precision kept where it pays.

Runtime precision: the KV cache is FP8 e4m3, while the recurrent (Mamba / GDN) state stays BF16.
Measured throughput

Single stream, greedy, thinking off, 320 tokens.

Decode speed varies by task but clusters around a 43.9 tok/s median. Concurrency scales nearly linearly to ×4, then saturates. Speculative decoding runs the MTP head at depth 3 over a reduced 47,149-token draft vocabulary.
